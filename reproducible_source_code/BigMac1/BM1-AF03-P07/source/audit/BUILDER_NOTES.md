# Builder notes

The strongest proved endpoint is the finite theorem for every alphabet of
cardinality at most eight. Its dependencies are the formal definitions, the
staircase finiteness lemma, exhaustive shape-first enumeration, exact Hecke
transitions, published Algorithm 1/Theorem 3.1, and the order-convexity identity
\(S=\uparrow S\cap\downarrow S\) relative to points lying between endpoints.

The natural general route is the prescribed-cover filling lemma recorded in
`proof/structural_reduction.md`. It is equivalent to the general conjecture,
but no K-jdt construction controlling a prescribed one-box shape change has
been proved. The builder therefore makes no general claim.
