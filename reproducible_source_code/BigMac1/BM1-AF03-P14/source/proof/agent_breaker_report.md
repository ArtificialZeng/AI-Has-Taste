# Counterexample-hunter report: (Q_5) central sections

## Outcome and epistemic status

No non-diagonal locally definite critical point was found.  The strongest
full-support non-diagonal candidate found by two independent floating-point
searches was exactified instead as a saddle:

\[
 v=\frac{(1,1,t,t,t)}{\sqrt{2+3t^2}},\qquad
 t=\frac{24-\sqrt{69}}{39}=0.402394259925\ldots .
\]

Its criticality and Hessian signature ((1+,3-)) are checked by a fail-closed,
standard-library-only verifier which reconstructs the box-spline chamber from
all 32 sign vectors.  This is an exact new *project artifact*, not a claim of
novelty in the literature.  It is evidence for the conjecture, but it is not a
classification of all (Q_5) critical directions and is not a proof that all
non-diagonal critical points are saddles.

The known (Q_4) baseline ((1,1,1/2,1/2)), proportional to
((2,2,1,1)), was also independently reconstructed and exactly certified as
a saddle.  No Lean, Coq, Isabelle, or other proof assistant was used.

## Independent formula and evaluator

Let (U_i\sim\operatorname{Unif}[-1/2,1/2]) independently and let
(a_i>0).  Coarea gives

\[
 \operatorname{Vol}_{m-1}(Q_m\cap a^\perp)
 =\lVert a\rVert_2 f_{\sum a_iU_i}(0).
\]

Successive convolution of the interval indicators, or differentiating the
inclusion-exclusion formula for the distribution function, gives

\[
 F_m(a)=\frac{\lVert a\rVert_2}{(m-1)!\prod_i a_i}
 \sum_{S\subseteq[m]}(-1)^{|S|}
 \left(\frac12\sum_i a_i-\sum_{i\in S}a_i\right)_+^{m-1}.
\]

Write the positive-part numerator as (G).  In an open chamber,

\[
 \nabla\log F_m=\frac{a}{\lVert a\rVert^2}
 +\frac{\nabla G}{G}-\left(\frac1{a_i}\right)_i,
\]

and

\[
 \nabla^2\log F_m=
 \frac I{\lVert a\rVert^2}-\frac{2aa^T}{\lVert a\rVert^4}
 +\frac{\nabla^2G}{G}-\frac{(\nabla G)(\nabla G)^T}{G^2}
 +\operatorname{diag}(a_i^{-2}).
\]

At a critical point this last matrix, restricted to (a^\perp), has the
same signature as the spherical Hessian of the section volume because
(F_m>0).  For (m=5), the fourth positive-part power makes the formula
(C^3) across subset-sum walls, so its gradient and Hessian remain valid on
those walls.  Zero-coordinate strata were not evaluated by dividing by
(\prod a_i); they were searched separately using their support size.

The formula evaluator was cross-checked against a geometrically independent
routine: enumerate all cube-edge/hyperplane intersections, project to an
orthonormal basis of (a^\perp), and compute the convex-hull volume.  Across
206 cases (50 random cases for every support (m=2,3,4,5), plus symmetric
and critical test cases), the maximum absolute discrepancy was
(2.220446049250313\times10^{-15}), and the maximum relative discrepancy was
(1.6883021373544264\times10^{-15}).  This validates implementation
consistency only; it is not used as exact proof.

## Exact reproduction of the (Q_4) baseline

At (a=(1,1,1/2,1/2)), direct enumeration of the 16 sign vectors gives six
active vectors, four wall vectors, and

\[
 G=\frac54,\qquad F_4(a)=\frac{5\sqrt{10}}{12},\qquad
 \nabla\log F_4(a)=0.
\]

The tangent space decomposes under (S_2\times S_2) into the three mutually
orthogonal lines generated by

\[
 (1,-1,0,0),\quad(0,0,1,-1),\quad(1/2,1/2,-1,-1).
\]

The corresponding exact eigenvalues of (\nabla^2\log F_4) are

\[
 -1,\qquad-\frac25,\qquad\frac15.
\]

Thus this direction is exactly a saddle.  The certificate and verifier are
`results/breaker_q4_baseline_certificate.json` and
`src/breaker_verify_q4_baseline.py`.

## Exactification of the (Q_5) non-diagonal candidate

On the (S_2\times S_3)-symmetric ray (a=(1,1,t,t,t)), the chamber
(0<t<2/3) has

\[
 G=\frac34t^3(32-13t),\qquad
 F_5(1,1,t,t,t)=\frac{(32-13t)\sqrt{2+3t^2}}{32}.
\]

The critical equation reduces to

\[
 39t^2-48t+13=0.
\]

Only its smaller root lies in this chamber:

\[
 t=\frac{24-\sqrt{69}}{39}\in
 \left(\frac{201197}{500000},\frac{80479}{200000}\right)
 \subset(0,2/3).
\]

Permutation symmetry makes the full five-component logarithmic gradient
constant inside each coordinate block; scale invariance supplies the one
remaining relation.  The exact verifier goes further and recomputes all five
components in (\mathbb Q[t]/(39t^2-48t+13)), obtaining zero componentwise.

The tangent space has the orthogonal invariant decomposition

\[
 \langle(1,-1,0,0,0)\rangle
 \oplus\{(0,0,y_1,y_2,y_3):\sum y_i=0\}
 \oplus\langle(3t,3t,-2,-2,-2)\rangle.
\]

On these summands the exact logarithmic-Hessian eigenvalues are respectively

\[
 -\frac{10462+944\sqrt{69}}{22165}<0,
\]

\[
 -\frac{1106+32\sqrt{69}}{22165}<0
 \quad\text{(multiplicity two)},
\]

and

\[
 \frac{-46+48\sqrt{69}}{1705}>0.
\]

Therefore the spherical Hessian has exact signature ((1+,3-)).  In
particular this algebraic non-diagonal critical section is not a local
extremum.

The machine-readable input format is deliberately small:

* schema identifier;
* minimal polynomial in constant-first integer coefficient order;
* two rational isolating endpoints encoded as strings;
* literal vector pattern using the symbol `t`;
* expected active-sign count and expected signature.

The verifier does not accept stored objective, gradient, or Hessian values.
It reconstructs the chamber, (G,\nabla G,\nabla^2G), stationarity, invariant
splitting, and all decisive signs.  Unknown, missing, or altered fields are
rejected.  Files: `results/breaker_saddle_certificate.json` and
`src/breaker_verify_saddle.py`.

## Search coverage and retained evidence

All numerical results below are discovery evidence only.

### Full-support and lower-support open chambers

`src/breaker_search.py` fixed scale by (a_1=1), used log-ratios in
([-7,0]), evaluated exact chamber jets in float64, and solved the (m-1)
independent stationary equations with their analytic Jacobian.  Coordinates
were canonically sorted only after solving.  Two runs used:

* seed `140829`, 1,000 random starts per support;
* seed `314159265`, 10,000 random starts per support.

Both runs retained only the following reliable types (up to support and
permutation):

* support four: the diagonal and ((2,2,1,1)), the latter indefinite;
* support five: the diagonal and the algebraic ((1,1,t,t,t)) above, the
  latter indefinite.

The support-three diagonal has zero Hessian.  The raw JSON contains many
near-diagonal entries which passed a loose gradient threshold because the
stationary equations are highly degenerate there.  They are numerical
convergence artifacts, not distinct roots, and must not be counted.  Exact
elimination in the ordered support-three triangle chamber, with scale
(a_1=1\), gives for the two derivative numerators (p(x,y),q(x,y))

\[
 \operatorname{Res}_y(p,q)
 =-64x^2(x-1)^6(x^2+x+1)^3.
\]

For (1\ge x\ge y>0), this forces (x=1), and substitution forces (y=1).
In the dominant-coordinate chamber the formula is
(F_3(a,b,c)=\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}/a), whose positive (b,c) derivatives
exclude an interior critical point.  On (a=b+c) the same first derivative
excludes criticality.  This exact elimination was derived in the breaker
notes but does not yet have a separate serialized no-import certificate.

For support two, the ordered open chamber has
(F_2(a,b)=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}/a), hence no interior critical direction.  The wall
(a=b) is the diagonal cusp.  Support one is the coordinate diagonal.

### Repeated-coordinate strata

`src/breaker_strata_search.py` enumerated every integer partition of each
support (m=2,3,4,5), every assignment of its multiplicities to coordinate
value groups, and solved the symmetry-reduced equations with 300 starts per
ordered multiplicity composition (seed `99140829`).  Exact permutation
symmetry means a zero of the reduced group gradients is a zero of the full
gradient.  After discounting the support-three near-diagonal degeneracy, it
found the same support-four and support-five types above and no further type.
This does not exclude all-distinct roots exactly.

On the (2+3) stratum specifically, the other ordered chamber
(2/3<t<1) has derivative numerator

\[
 2(t-1)(3t^5-9t^4-8t^3+40t^2-48t+16).
\]

Numerical root isolation finds no quintic root in ((2/3,1)); this particular
last statement remains an exactification target unless a Sturm certificate is
added.  The endpoint (t=1) is the full diagonal.

### Subset-sum walls

Every full-support wall is permutation-equivalent to a (1|4) or (2|3)
partition.  `src/breaker_wall_search.py` parameterized the positive
coordinates on each side by independent softmax weights with total (1/2),
then minimized the complete five-component ambient gradient.  Seed
`77140829`, 2,000 starts per wall type, and logit bounds ([-10,10]) were
used.

No positive critical point was located.  The smallest (2|3) residual was
(1.7882\times10^{-5}) at a point whose smallest normalized coordinate was
(1.4356\times10^{-5}); it is visibly converging to the support-four
((2,2,1,1)) saddle, not to a full-support wall critical point.  The best
interior symmetric (2|3) wall point, proportional to
((3,3,2,2,2)), had gradient norm about (0.1278).  The (1|4) symmetric
point proportional to ((4,1,1,1,1)) had gradient norm
(0.8944271909999165).  This is not an exact wall no-go proof, and wall
intersections have not been exhaustively certified by CAD or elimination.

## Reproducibility

Environment recorded by the artifacts:

* Python 3.13.5;
* NumPy 2.2.3;
* SciPy 1.15.3;
* platform time zone inherited from the project environment; all RNG seeds
  are explicit above.

Commands from the project root:

```bash
python src/breaker_geometric_crosscheck.py --random 50 --seed 512140829 \
  --output experiments/breaker_geometric_crosscheck_seed512140829_n50.json
python src/breaker_search.py --starts 10000 --seed 314159265 \
  --output experiments/breaker_search_seed314159265_s10000.json
PYTHONPATH=src python src/breaker_strata_search.py --starts 300 --seed 99140829 \
  --output experiments/breaker_strata_seed99140829_s300.json
PYTHONPATH=src python src/breaker_wall_search.py --starts 2000 --seed 77140829 \
  --output experiments/breaker_wall_seed77140829_s2000.json
python src/breaker_verify_q4_baseline.py \
  results/breaker_q4_baseline_certificate.json
python src/breaker_verify_saddle.py results/breaker_saddle_certificate.json
python src/breaker_test_verifiers.py \
  --output experiments/breaker_verifier_tests.json
```

Both valid certificates were accepted.  Five destructive-input tests
(changed polynomial, missing field, wrong isolating interval, changed
signature, and changed baseline vector) were all rejected.  The complete
test log is `experiments/breaker_verifier_tests.json`.

## Remaining gaps

1. There is no exhaustive exact enumeration of all full-support open
   chambers.  An all-distinct algebraic critical point outside the basins of
   the numerical solver is not excluded.
2. There is no exact no-solution certificate for the (1|4) and (2|3)
   subset-sum walls or their intersections.
3. The support-three resultant calculation should be bound to a serialized
   resultant/Bézout or Gröbner certificate before it is used as a released
   theorem component.
4. The quintic sign on (2/3<t<1) should receive a Sturm certificate if the
   symmetric-stratum classification is used in a proof.
5. Local behavior transverse to zero-coordinate strata was not classified in
   full.  This does not rescue the non-diagonal support-four candidate,
   because it already has both signs within its own support, but it matters
   for a complete list of diagonal extrema and for any degenerate boundary
   argument.

Accordingly the breaker branch supports only the statement “no
counterexample found in the recorded search, and one natural non-diagonal
full-support candidate is an exact saddle.”  It does not justify `PROVED` or
`DISPROVED` for the original (Q_5) classification.
