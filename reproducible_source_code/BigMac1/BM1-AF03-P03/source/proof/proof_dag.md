# Proof dependency graph

1. **D1 (source definitions).** `problem/formal_statement.md` reconstructs the superalgebra, action, invariant ideal, modified Motzkin paths, generalized \(\alpha\)-sequence, and candidate set.
2. **L1 (orbit-sum generation).** Invariants of the super place-permutation action are generated by one-slot power sums.  Proof: Reynolds spanning plus induction over kernel partitions of slot maps.
3. **L2 (finite truncation).** Newton identities and the characteristic polynomial reduce every one-slot power sum to the displayed \(4n\) generators.
4. **L3 (ideal equality).** L1 and L2 imply that the full positive-invariant Hilbert ideal equals the exact \(4n\)-generator ideal (`proof/generator_lemma.md`).
5. **C1 (finite exact standard basis).** From D1 and L3, the serialized \(n=5\) ideal is exactly the source quotient ideal.  Exact `twostd` gives quotient dimension 1920.
6. **C2 (candidate rank).** Independently reconstructed Definition 3.1 gives 1920 candidates; exact normal-form coordinates have rank 1920.
7. **A1 (independent Artin--exterior audit).** Exact FLINT rational RREF verifies spanning and independence in all 396 blocks without using the standard-basis route.
8. **A2 (frozen no-import breaker).** A separate implementation reconstructs the Artin quotient, signed Reynolds ideal, and candidates from a small hash-bound configuration and verifies both block-rank identities over \(\mathbb Q\) in all 396 blocks.
9. **T1 (finite endpoint).** C1 and C2 imply \(B_5^{(1,2)}\) is a basis; A1 and A2 independently audit the same endpoint.  Scalar extension gives the source's complex-field endpoint.

No arrow in this DAG uses the conjectural dimension formula as an input: 1920 is obtained both as the enumerated candidate count and as the exact quotient dimension.
