# General-structure exploration after the certified (n=5) result

This file records exploratory consequences only.  Nothing here is used in the
(n=5) certificate, and the (n=6) line is not promoted to a theorem without
an independent certificate.

The structural proof in `proof/generator_lemma.md` is uniform in (n): the
Hilbert ideal is generated by the (4n) polarized power sums

\[
p_{r,1}\ (1\le r\le n),\qquad
p_{r,U}\ (0\le r<n,\ U\in\{\theta,\xi,\theta\xi\}).
\]

Using these generators over (mathbb Q), the same degree-product-order
Singular discovery route gives:

| (n) | (4n) generators | exact discovery quotient dimension | (2^{n-1}n!) | standard-basis size |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 12 | 24 | 24 | 19 |
| 4 | 16 | 192 | 192 | 59 |
| 5 | 20 | 1920 | 1920 | 271 |
| 6 | 24 | 23040 | 23040 | 1699 |

The (n=6) command was

```sh
python3 src/candidate_basis.py 6 --dimension-only | /opt/homebrew/bin/Singular -q
```

and returned

```text
n=6 generators=24 vdim=23040 std-size=1699
```

This is exact rational discovery evidence for the predicted dimension at
(n=6), but it does not check the rank of the 23040 candidate residue
classes.  Therefore it does not prove the (n=6) basis case.  The standard
basis sizes (19,59,271,1699) also do not by themselves exhibit a certified
initial-ideal recurrence.  The only uniform theorem extracted here is the
(4n)-generator reduction; a general candidate straightening recursion
remains open.
