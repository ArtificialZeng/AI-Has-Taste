## Conclusion

Lentfer's Conjecture 3.2 is true at (n=5): over every field of
characteristic zero, the 1920 residue classes in (B_5^{(1,2)}) form a basis
of (R_5^{(1,2)}).  Equivalently,

\[
\dim R_5^{(1,2)}=1920=|B_5^{(1,2)}|.
\]

This is the first case beyond the (n\leq4) verification reported by Lentfer.
No earlier public settlement was found in the searches recorded through
2026-08-29, but that novelty statement is scoped rather than global.

## Scope and limitations

- The result is the finite (n=5) endpoint, not a proof for arbitrary (n).
- An exact discovery run gives the predicted quotient dimension 23040 at
  (n=6), but no candidate-rank certificate was run there; it is not claimed
  as an (n=6) basis theorem.
- No general initial-ideal or straightening recurrence was certified.
- The candidates need not be literal standard monomials for the declared
  degree-product order; the theorem is the order-independent basis statement.
- The recorded novelty search could not inspect a cited 2026 dissertation or
  authenticated GitHub file contents.  It found no settlement in its stated
  scope and makes no worldwide absence claim.
- This work has not been peer reviewed and makes no claim of journal
  acceptance.

No Lean, Coq, Isabelle, or other proof assistant was used.  The formalized
endpoint is therefore none; the theorem is supported by the written reduction
and exact rational/integer certificates described above.
