# Proof audit

Audit date: 2026-08-30.

## Reconstructed definitions

An additive square is a factor \(UV\) with \(|U|=|V|>0\) and equal integer
sums. With \(S_n=\sum_{j<n}a_j\), this is equivalent to
\(S_i+S_{i+2\ell}=2S_{i+\ell}\). The empty word and one-letter words are ASF.
For a fixed finite alphabet \(A\), \(g(A)=\infty\) is equivalent to existence
of an infinite ASF word by König's lemma; the fixed-\(A\) qualifier is
essential.

## Structural reductions

1. Translation cancels because compared blocks have equal length, and a
   nonzero scalar multiplier preserves equality and inequality. Thus
   primitive minimum-zero normalization and reflection are valid.
2. Direct enumeration of \(0<a<b<c\leq5\), gcd one, followed by reflection
   canonicalization gives exactly the seven certificate alphabets: one at
   height 3, two at height 4, and four at height 5.
3. Appending a letter to an ASF word changes no old factor. Hence every new
   additive square must be a suffix, and checking all positive suffix
   half-lengths generates exactly the ASF prefix tree.

## Finite-census theorem

- Lower bounds: each reported maximum-depth witness is regenerated by the
  independent evaluator and passes every exact suffix test.
- Upper bounds: both exhaustive traversals test every alphabet letter at every
  accepted prefix and terminate with no child beyond the reported maximum
  depth.
- Completeness of scope: the fail-closed coordinator now fixes both the scope
  string and the exact ordered seven-alphabet list. Deleting a whole record is
  rejected.
- Binding: for each row the verifier compares nodes, leaves, maximum depth,
  maximizer count, a SHA-256 digest of every ordered maximizer, and membership
  of the serialized witness.

Therefore the seven exact \(g(A)\) values and their tree statistics follow.
This theorem has no unresolved major or fatal gap within its stated
height-at-most-five scope.

## Restricted morphic theorem

After relabeling a prolongable symbol as 0, a four-symbol 2-uniform
endomorphism has \(h(0)=0x\) and seven remaining free image positions. This is
exactly \(4^7\) cases, including nonprimitive and letter-omitting maps. For
each case a fixed-point prefix is nested under iteration. Independent direct
multiset comparison finds an abelian square by exclusive endpoint 19. Equal
Parikh vectors imply equal sum under every integer weighting.

The theorem does not cover a coding from more states, a nonuniform morphism,
or uniform length at least 3. No gap remains within the stated four-state
2-uniform class.

## Disproof-route audit

The modular van der Waerden idea is incomplete: congruence of a second
difference modulo \(m\) does not imply zero because the progression gap and
therefore the magnitude bound depend on \(m\). The bounded scalar-projection
screen is diagnostic only. Neither route supports a global impossibility
claim.

There is also no bound on \(g(A)\) depending only on four-letter alphabet
size. A length-\(N-1\) prefix of a four-symbol abelian-square-free word,
weighted by \(1,N,N^2,N^3\), is ASF by signed base-\(N\) uniqueness.
