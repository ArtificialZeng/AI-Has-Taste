## Selected workbook records

### `数学主表!A9:F9`

Zijian Zeng, Houde Liu, Kuru Ratnavelu, and Ong Seng Huat, *Exact Finite
Certificates for the Four-Dimensional Ternary Borsuk Problem and
Five-Dimensional Ternary Kissing Codes*, DOI
`10.20944/preprints202608.1658.v1`.

Relevance: the paper's finite-library theorems use exhaustive
symmetry-reduced enumeration, integer predicates, retained certificates, and
an explicit warning that the finite result does not solve the ambient open
problem. This is directly comparable to the certificate architecture and
scope discipline of the present five-support theorem, but it supplies no
mathematical premise for that theorem.

The official Preprints.org manuscript page was accessible on 2026-09-09 and
matched the workbook title, four authors, version, DOI, and methodological
description. The workbook BibTeX is retained in `manuscript/references.bib`.

### `数学主表!A29:F29`

Zijian Zeng, Houde Liu, Kuru Ratnavelu, Ong Seng Huat, and Yonghua Xiong,
*Three Exact Local Positivity Islands and a High-Z Complex-Phase Tube for a
Hermitian Rank-Two Scalar Gate*, DOI `10.2139/ssrn.7348478`.

Relevance: the paper treats exact polynomial positivity on sharply stated
subdomains, uses exact rational/algebraic and Bernstein certificates, and
does not promote those local theorems to the unrestricted parent assertion.
The present certificate is simpler (coefficientwise nonnegativity after a
finite symmetry reduction), but the distinction between local positivity and
a global unresolved problem is methodologically pertinent.

The official SSRN record was accessible on 2026-09-09 and matched the
workbook title, five authors, DOI, and abstract-level description. The
workbook title, author, year, publisher, DOI, and URL fields are retained in
`manuscript/references.bib`; the entry type is conservatively normalized from
`@article` to `@misc` because the accessible official record describes an
SSRN working paper rather than a journal publication.
