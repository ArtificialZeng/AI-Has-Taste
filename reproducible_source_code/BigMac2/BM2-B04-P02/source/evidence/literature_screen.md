# Bounded primary-literature comparison

Search date: 2026-09-06 (Asia/Shanghai).  This is a bounded comparison, not
a claim of priority or an exhaustive bibliography.

## Primary sources inspected

1. S. Akbari, Fu-Tao Hu, and Ya-Yang Liu, *Positive and Negative Square
   Energies of 2-Connected Graphs*, arXiv:2609.04069v1,
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04069> and the local file
   `literature/Akbari-Hu-Liu-2609.04069v1.pdf`.  The arXiv history showed only
   v1, submitted 2026-09-03.  Theorem 3.4 proves the non-strict bound
   \(s^+(G)\ge n\) for 2-connected noncycles and proves strictness under the
   additional hypothesis that a maximum-degree vertex lies in no triangle.
   It does not state universal strictness or classify the equality layer.

2. Yinchen Liu, Quanyu Tang, and Shengtong Zhang, *The positive and negative
   square-energy conjecture*, arXiv:2607.18031v1,
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18031>.  Its stated result is the connected-graph
   endpoint bound \(\min\{s^+(G),s^-(G)\}\ge n-1\), one dependency used by the
   source paper and by the present proof.  Its abstract does not assert the
   2-connected strict \(>n\) theorem.

3. Fu-Tao Hu, Ya-Yang Liu, and Yi Wang, *Extremal graphs for a conjecture on
   the square energy of graphs*, arXiv:2608.17329v1,
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17329>.  It classifies equality at the different
   endpoint \(s^+(G)=n-1\) (exactly trees) and \(s^-(G)=n-1\) (trees and
   complete graphs).  No occurrence of “2-connected” or a theorem
   \(s^+(G)>n\) was found in the arXiv record/full-text search used here.

4. S. Akbari, H. Kumar, B. Mohar, S. Pragada, and S. Zhang, *Refinement of a
   conjecture on positive square energy of graphs*, arXiv:2506.07264,
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07264>.  It proposes \(s^+(G)\ge n\) for
   connected graphs with at least \(n+1\) edges and proves cases including
   claw-free and diameter-two graphs; the abstract does not give the present
   equality classification.

## Searches and conclusion

The web/arXiv searches included the exact source title and combinations of
“positive square energy”, “2-connected noncycle/non-cycle”, “strict”,
“equality”, and the formulas \(s^+(G)=n\) and \(s^+(G)>n\).  The exact-topic
results led back to arXiv:2609.04069; the other primary results above concern
the \(n-1\) endpoint or different graph classes.

No equivalent universal strictness theorem was located in this bounded search.
Because the source preprint was only three days old and general web indexing is
incomplete, this supports only the narrow statement that the theorem in
`evidence/strictness_proof.md` is a genuine strengthening not present in the
inspected sources.  It does **not** establish global novelty, priority, or
publication-worthiness; those remain for independent review.
