## DM07-01 Pure periodicity for the fixed subtraction-game shape `(2,5)`

Use exactly the definitions and normalizations of Manabe,
*Purely Periodic Three-move Subtraction Games*, arXiv:2609.05358v1.
For an integer `c>5`, let `S={2,5,c}`, let `G_S(n)` be its Sprague--Grundy
sequence, and put `rho=c mod 7`. Exclude the separately treated additive point
`c=7`. Prove or disprove the following fixed-shape specialization of
Conjecture 50:

`G_S` is periodic from `n=0` if and only if

`rho in Theta_7 union Theta_{c+2} union Theta_{c+5}`,

where the three admissible-angle sets are those defined in the source for
`a=2,b=5`. In the positive case, the least period must equal the least member
of `{7,c+2,c+5}` for which the angle is admissible, with any proper-divisor
exception handled exactly as in the source.

The source proves the forward construction (admissible implies purely
periodic) in general. The requested contribution is the missing necessity for
this one shape, or an exact counterexample. Computations over finitely many
`c` values are evidence only unless they are converted to a finite-state or
other all-`c` certificate. Preserve the distinction between outcome
periodicity and full nim-value periodicity.

Primary source: arXiv:2609.05358v1, Theorem 32, Conjecture 50,
Conjectures 54--55, and the status discussion on PDF pp. 3--4 and 33--35.
