# bigMac-00010-p03 — Inverse-weight LLY flow on the triangle

## Immutable source statement

Apply the same inverse-edge-weight transition probabilities, shortest-path
metric, Lin--Lu--Yau curvature, and normalized continuous Ricci-flow convention
as arXiv:2609.04671v1 to `C3`.  Starting from every strictly positive triple of
edge lengths, determine the global omega-limit set.  In particular, prove or
disprove convergence of normalized weights to the uniform metric, and classify
all interior fixed points, invariant faces, and possible boundary degenerations.

## Primary boundary

Bai and Hua, *Curvature Diffusion of Inverse-weight Lin--Lu--Yau Ricci Flow on
Finite Trees*, arXiv:2609.04671v1, Introduction and Theorems 1.1--1.3.  The
source treats finite trees and explicitly says behavior on graphs with cycles
remains open.

## Permitted contribution boundary

A complete piecewise-exact phase portrait, a proof of the asserted global
convergence, or an exact positive initial metric with a different limiting
behavior.  Floating ODE trajectories alone are insufficient.
