# Precise reading of the `D_4` Condition-A problem

## Ambient monoid and notation

Let `M` be the positive Artin--Tits monoid of type `D_4` with atom set

`S = {σ'_0, σ_0, σ_1, σ_2}`,

whose Coxeter graph has the three edges `σ'_0—σ_1`, `σ_0—σ_1`, and
`σ_1—σ_2`, all labelled `3`. Thus adjacent atoms satisfy `sts=tst`, and
nonadjacent atoms commute. Let `G` be the group of fractions of `M`. Let
`Δ` be the standard minimal Garside element, equivalently the left/right lcm
of `S`; the source gives the representative

`Δ = σ_0 σ'_0 (σ_1 σ_0 σ'_0 σ_1)
          (σ_2 σ_1 σ_0 σ'_0 σ_1 σ_2)`.

For `T ⊆ S`, write `M_T` for the standard parabolic submonoid generated by
`T`. For `g,h ∈ M`, `h` right-divides `g` if `g=uh` for some `u∈M`.
The right `T`-tail `τ_T(g)` is the unique maximal right divisor of `g` lying
in `M_T`.

Fix

`S_1 = {σ'_0, σ_1}` and `S_2 = {σ_0, σ_1, σ_2}`.

These are proper subsets with `S_1 ∪ S_2=S`; both induced graphs are
connected, so `(S_2,S_1)` is a proper irreducible covering.

For `i≥1`, let `π(i)=1` for odd `i` and `π(i)=2` for even `i`. The
`(S_2,S_1)`-alternating normal form of `g∈M` is the unique sequence
`[g]=(g_n,…,g_1)` such that

- `g=g_n⋯g_1` and `g_i∈M_{S_{π(i)}}`;
- `g_n≠1` (apart from the prescribed normal form of `1`); and
- `g_i=τ_{S_{π(i)}}(g_n⋯g_i)` for every `i`.

Its depth is

`dpt(g) = #{i : g_i ∉ M_{S_1}} = floor(n/2)`.

For nontrivial `g`, let `L(h,S)={s∈S : h=su for some u∈M}` and define

`L*(g,S) = L(g_n,S)`,

where `g_n` is the leftmost factor of `[g]`. All normal forms, tails, depths,
and `L*` below are relative to this fixed ordered covering.

The Garside automorphism is `Φ_Δ(x)=ΔxΔ^{-1}`. Since the type is `D_4`
(even `D_r`), `Δ` is central and hence `Φ_Δ` is the identity. Define

`S_1^⊥={s∈S : sx=xs for every x∈S_1}` and
`S_2⊟S_1=S_2\(S_1∪S_1^⊥)`.

Here `S_1^⊥=∅`: each atom fails to commute with either `σ'_0` or `σ_1`.
Consequently

`Φ_Δ(S_2⊟S_1)=S_2⊟S_1={σ_0,σ_2}`.

## Frozen target and quantifiers

The first assertion is the exact set equality

`L*(Δ,S) = {σ_0,σ_2} = Φ_Δ(S_2⊟S_1)`.

The claimed consequence is the all-powers statement

`∀ p∈Z_{≥1} ∀ t∈Z_{≥1},
 dpt(Δ^{pt})-1 = t(dpt(Δ^p)-1).`

For a fixed `p`, the universally quantified equality is Definition 4.1's
Condition A with respect to `Δ^p`. The source's Corollary 4.7 reduces the
case `p=1` to the displayed `L*` equality because this covering is
`Δ`-regular and `Δ`-fixed. Condition A for `Δ` then implies the formula for
every base power `Δ^p` by substituting `pt` and `p` in the `p=1` formula.

A disproof may therefore consist of either (a) an exact alternating-normal-
form/divisibility certificate showing the displayed `L*` set equality is
false, or (b) exact integers `p≥1,t≥2` for which the depth equality fails,
with exact normal-form certificates for both depths. The phrase "least
`(p,t)`" in `source.md` does not specify an order on pairs; for bounded
search and reporting only, use lexicographic order (least `p`, then least
`t`) and disclose that convention. This ambiguity does not change the truth
of either universally quantified assertion.

No claim for `D_r` with `r>4`, no Condition B claim, and no left-orderability
conclusion is in scope. Finite checks of powers or depths are evidence only,
not a proof of the universal statement.

## Source status, nearest result, and proposed delta

Fromentin--Godelle, arXiv:2609.04757v1 (submitted 2026-09-04), Section 4.3,
p. 19, explicitly conjectures this equality for even `D_r` at `i=1`; the
present problem is exactly its smallest case `r=4`. In the same paper,
Lemma 3.14 supplies the inclusion `L*(Δ,S)⊆Φ_Δ(S_2⊟S_1)`, Lemma 4.10 marks
this family as exceptional, Proposition 4.11 does not settle it, and
Corollary 4.7 supplies the Condition-A implication once equality is proved.
The current arXiv record and v1 HTML were inspected on 2026-09-07:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04757 and
https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04757v1. An exact-assertion arXiv search found no
separate resolution, but this limited search is not a priority or exhaustive
open-status claim. Source status is therefore `open-supported` relative to
the cited v1.

Target contribution: nearest prior result = the source's inclusion and
conditional criterion; proposed delta = settle the missing reverse inclusion
in `D_4` (or give an exact countercertificate); verification route = compute
and independently certify the alternating normal form of the explicit
length-12 word for `Δ`, including every maximal right-tail step, then certify
the atom set left-dividing its leftmost factor.
