# bigMac-00008-p02 — 一般二态 unitary coin 的 time-8 Hadamard rigidity

## Immutable source statement

Use exactly the positive-sojourn-time and conditional-on-return convention of
arXiv:2609.05033v1 and fix

`phi_* = (1,i)^T / sqrt(2)`.

Classify every matrix `U in U(2)` for which the time-eight conditional
distribution is supported on `k=2,4,6` with probability `1/3` at each of these
three values.  Determine, after quotienting only by the global phase, diagonal
unitary transformations, and basis equivalences that genuinely preserve this
fixed experiment, whether all such coins are of Hadamard type.  If not, give
an exact non-Hadamard family and state the correct classification.

## Primary boundary

*Hadamard Rigidity of Positive Sojourn Time Distributions for Rotation Coins*,
arXiv:2609.05033v1, Theorem 3.1 and Section 5.  The theorem proves time-eight
rigidity only for the real rotation family; Section 5 explicitly proposes the
extension to general two-state unitary coins.  Equivalences may not be assumed
to preserve the fixed initial state without proof.

## Permitted contribution boundary

An exact parameter classification, including all degenerate and zero-return
faces, or an exact counterfamily to the Hadamard-only claim.  Floating sampling
or a Gröbner output without real-branch and equivalence analysis is not enough.
