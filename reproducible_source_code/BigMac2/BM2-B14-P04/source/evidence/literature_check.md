# Bounded primary-literature check

Checked 2026-09-07.

The current arXiv record for Dürrschnabel, Hodor, Micek, Stumme, and Trotter,
*Relative Dimension of Posets*, is arXiv:2609.05166v1, submitted 2026-09-04:
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05166>.

The HTML full text at <https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05166v1> defines local
realizers and relative dimension in Sections 1.3--1.4. Figure 1 displays the
three PLEs used here as the upper certificate. In the proof of Theorem 5 the
text explicitly states only

```text
rdim(B_3) <= 2
```

before applying the product inequality. No matching finite lower certificate
for $B_3$ was found in that text. Its open-problems section asks for the
asymptotic behavior of the Boolean lattices, not the separate exact value at
$n=3$.

Focused web/arXiv-index searches for the exact strings `rdim(B_3)` and
`relative dimension` together with `B_3` returned no distinct mathematical
source beyond arXiv:2609.05166v1. Because the notion and preprint are new and
indexing can be incomplete, this is a bounded comparison only, not proof of
priority or open status. The defensible contribution claim is therefore:
relative to the inspected v1 text, the present artifacts supply the missing
exact lower certificate.
