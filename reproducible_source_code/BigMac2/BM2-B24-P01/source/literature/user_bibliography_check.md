Two records were selected, both for a narrow methodological comparison rather
than as support for any theorem in rational dynamics.

1. Worksheet `数学主表`, row 2: Zijian Zeng, Houde Liu, and Kurunathan
   Ratnavelu, *A Counterexample to the Tang--Zhang Schatten Norm Conjecture and
   Sharp Positive Results*, *Preprints* (2026), DOI
   `10.20944/preprints202608.1067.v1`.  The workbook abstract explicitly says
   that its comparison is certified by strict rational inequalities.  This is
   genuinely relevant to the proof-carrying strict-inequality style of the
   present orbit certificate, but not to its dynamical content.

2. Worksheet `数学主表`, row 13: Zijian Zeng, *Fixed Convex-Lens Spectral
   Constants: Möbius Reduction, Sharp Model Theorems, and Angle-Dependent
   Bounds*, *Preprints* (2026), DOI `10.20944/preprints202608.1272.v1`.  The
   workbook abstract describes exact rational certificates in a
   complex-analytic spectral-set problem.  The relation is methodological;
   the record supplies no fact about Chebyshev iteration or Fatou components.
