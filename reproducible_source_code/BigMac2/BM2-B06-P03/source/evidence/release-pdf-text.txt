3    Relation to the motivating conjecture
Equation (1) shows that the k = 4 series appearing in the source conjecture is f216 /f132 , whereas
the series tested in (2) is f28 /f116 . Theorem 1 therefore resolves only the stated k = 2 problem. It
neither proves nor disproves the k = 4 congruence in [1, Conjecture 7.1]. The source comparison
of partition congruences is limited to that directly motivating paper; no claim of an exhaustive
literature search or of priority is made.
    The use of generating-function coefficient analysis alongside separate exact finite diagnostics
provides a methodological comparison with Zeng’s study of distinct cycle lengths [2]. Here the
finite calculation produces a counterexample at one index; it is not used to infer a pattern at all
indices. No result on cycle lengths is needed for the partition calculation above.
