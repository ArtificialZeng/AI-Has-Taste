# bigMac-00011-p04 — Covariance of competing stopping mechanisms in the three-ball

## Immutable source statement

In the unit three-dimensional ball with diffusion constant `D=1` and initial
point uniformly distributed in the ball, use exactly the bulk stopping rate
`p>0`, surface-local-time stopping rate `q>0`, stopping time `T`, and stopped
local time `L` defined in arXiv:2609.05247v1.  Prove that

`Cov(T,L)>0`

for every `(p,q) in (0,infinity)^2`, equivalently that the source's Appendix-F
quantity `C(p,q | circle)` is positive, or give an exact parameter
counterexample and classify the zero set in the positive quadrant.

## Primary boundary

Ye, *Diffusion under competing bulk and surface stopping mechanisms*,
arXiv:2609.05247v1, Appendices D and F, especially equations (F4)--(F5) and
Figure 8.  The source gives a closed formula and reports positivity over the
explored parameter range, but does not prove its sign on the full quadrant.

## Permitted contribution boundary

An exact sign proof for the Appendix-F numerator
`xi_1(p)+q xi_2(p)` for all positive parameters, or an exact counterexample
and zero-set classification.  A sampled heat map or floating evaluation on a
bounded box is not a proof of full-quadrant positivity.
