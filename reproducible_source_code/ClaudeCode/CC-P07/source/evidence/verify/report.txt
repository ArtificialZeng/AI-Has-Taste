1. WHAT IS CHECKED, AND WHY IT SUFFICES
--------------------------------------
Claim: for every sequence x of 9 distinct cards there are 9 further cards
y such that the 18-card deal x+y is winnable.

Witness files hold 18-card deals. For a deal r and a prefix length k the
verifier checks: (i) r is 18 distinct cards in 0..51 (not the 255 sentinel);
(ii) r is winnable by the verifier's own solver; (iii) the canonical pattern
of r's first k cards is computed. Then it checks that the set of canonical
patterns of winnable records equals the set of ALL canonical k-patterns,
enumerated independently.

Soundness: compatibility (same suit or same rank) is preserved by any
permutation of the 4 suits combined with any permutation of the 13 ranks,
so such a relabelling maps winnable deals to winnable deals. Given any
9-card x, let p be its canonical pattern and r a winnable record whose
prefix has pattern p. Both r[1..9] and x map to p by relabellings that are
injective on the suits/ranks used; extend to full permutations and compose
to get a relabelling g with g(r[1..9]) = x. Then g(r) is a winnable deal
of 18 distinct cards beginning with x. Hence no 9-card poison sequence.
(Dealing and moving may also be interleaved in the original game; this does
not change winnability, since moves only act leftward on existing piles and
any interleaved play can be postponed until all 18 cards are down.)

2. SOLVER (vsolve.h)
--------------------
State = sequence of top cards (6 bits each, packed into 128 bits). Moves
exactly as in ex. 484: if v[k]~v[k+1] delete v[k]; if v[l]~v[l+3] put v[l+3]
in place of v[l] and delete position l+3. Win iff length 1 reachable.
Depth-first search, moves tried left to right (at each k the 1-step capture
before the 3-step capture), with a per-deal hash table of states already
proven LOSING (a state is stored only after all its successors failed; the
table is an optimisation only - when full it would just stop inserting; it
never filled: "tablefull=0" everywhere). Length 2 is decided directly.
Pruning (sound necessary condition): the compatibility graph on the current
top cards must be connected - a card disappears only when a compatible card
is placed on it and survives, so each component keeps >= 1 card forever.

Independent cross-check solver (sanity.c, bfscheck.c): exhaustive
breadth-first enumeration of ALL reachable states, layer by layer (length
n, n-1, ..., 1), sorted and deduplicated, no pruning, no memo; win iff the
length-1 layer is nonempty.

4. NUMBER OF PREFIX PATTERNS (independent)
------------------------------------------
Three independent methods:
 (a) closed formula (patcount_formula.py): sequences using exactly a given a
     suits and b ranks are acted on freely by S_a x S_b, so
     #patterns(k) = sum_{a<=4,b<=13} Surj(a,b,k)/(a! b!), with Surj by
     inclusion-exclusion over unused rows/columns of the a x b grid;
 (b) recursive generation of canonical sequences (cover.c);
 (c) brute force over all ordered k-sequences of 52 cards, canonicalised and
