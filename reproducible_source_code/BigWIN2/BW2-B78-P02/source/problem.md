## Objects, order, and equivalence

Represent the ordered complexes by

\[
c_0=(0,0),\quad c_1=(1,0),\quad c_2=(0,1),\quad
c_3=(2,0),\quad c_4=(1,1),\quad c_5=(0,2),
\]

corresponding in order to \(0,X,Y,2X,X+Y,2Y\). A reaction is an ordered
pair \(\rho=(i,j)\), \(i\ne j\), meaning \(c_i\to c_j\). Reactions are
ordered lexicographically by \((i,j)\). A network is a subset \(E\) of these
30 reactions with \(q=|E|\in\{3,4\}\); all six complexes are allowed but
need not occur. Oppositely directed reactions may both occur. Because \(E\)
is a set, loops and repeated reactions are excluded.

The only isomorphism imposed is the species swap \(\tau:X\leftrightarrow Y\),
which sends complex indices \((0,1,2,3,4,5)\) to
\((0,2,1,5,4,3)\). Sort every reaction list and choose the lexicographically
smaller of \(E\) and \(\tau(E)\) as its canonical representative. There are
\(\binom{30}{3}+\binom{30}{4}=31{,}465\) labelled networks and 15,799
orbits before filtering (Burnside: 133 networks are fixed by the swap).

## Matrices and retention filters

For the sorted reactions \(\rho=(i,j)\), define the \(2\times q\) matrices

\[
(S^-)_{\bullet\rho}=c_i,\qquad
(S^+)_{\bullet\rho}=c_j,\qquad S=S^+-S^-.
\]

Let \(R_{\rm gen}\) be the \(q\times2\) matrix whose entry
\((\rho,m)\) is a distinct algebraically independent symbol
\(r_{\rho m}\) when \((S^-)_{m\rho}>0\), and is zero otherwise. Retain
exactly those networks satisfying all three conditions

1. \(\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}S=2\);
2. there is a \(v\in\mathbb R^q_{>0}\) with \(Sv=0\);
3. \(\det(SR_{\rm gen})\) is a nonzero polynomial.

Condition 2 concerns every reaction coordinate: the steady flux must be
strictly positive, not merely nonnegative. Condition 3 uses independent
symbols, even when two entries arise from the same reaction.

For a retained network, take any rational Gale matrix
\(G\in\mathbb Q^{q\times(q-2)}\) with \(SG=0\) and full column rank, and put

\[
A_{\rm gen}=[R_{\rm gen}\mid G].
\]

The first two rows of \(A_{\rm gen}^{-1}\), indexed by \(X,Y\), do not
depend on the chosen basis of \(\ker S\): replacing \(G\) by \(GB\) only
left-multiplies the inverse by \(\operatorname{diag}(I_2,B^{-1})\).

## Mass-action specialization and zero criterion

Let

\[
L_E=\{v\in\mathbb A^q:Sv=0\}.
\]

For \(v\in L_E\) and \(x=(x_X,x_Y)\), define

\[
R_{\rm MA}(v,x)=\operatorname{diag}(v)(S^-)^T
                 \operatorname{diag}(x)^{-1},\qquad
A_{\rm MA}(v,x)=[R_{\rm MA}(v,x)\mid G].
\]

This is exactly the classical-mass-action derivative matrix at a positive
steady state: for every \(v>0\) with \(Sv=0\) and every \(x>0\), the positive
rate constants \(k_\rho=v_\rho/x^{c_i}\) realize that pair, and conversely.
Moreover

\[
A_{\rm MA}(v,x)=A_{\rm MA}(v,(1,1))
\operatorname{diag}(x_X^{-1},x_Y^{-1},I_{q-2}),
\]

so normalization \(x=(1,1)\) preserves the zero/nonzero status of every
species row of the inverse.

For a species \(m\in\{X,Y\}\) and reaction \(\rho\in E\), set
\(g_{m\rho}=(A_{\rm gen}^{-1})_{m\rho}\). Its generic status is nonzero
exactly when its adjugate cofactor is a nonzero polynomial. The corresponding
mass-action entry is interpreted in the function field of \(L_E\), on the
nondegenerate locus \(\det A_{\rm MA}\ne0\). It is *identically zero on the
positive steady-state parameter variety* exactly when its specialized
cofactor reduces to zero modulo the prime linear ideal generated by the two
entries of \(Sv\), while \(\det A_{\rm MA}\) does not. Networks for which
the specialized determinant is identically zero have no generically defined
mass-action inverse and are not witnesses; they must be recorded separately.
Because Condition 2 gives a relatively open positive subset of \(L_E\), this
algebraic criterion agrees with vanishing at all positive nondegenerate
steady states.

## Quantified question and output order

The outer quantifier is existential over the finite retained class. Decide
whether there exist a retained canonical \(E\), a reaction
\(\rho\in E\), and \(m\in\{X,Y\}\) such that

\[
g_{m\rho}\ne0\quad\text{but}\quad
(A_{\rm MA}^{-1})_{m\rho}=0
\]

identically in the sense above. Search canonical network reaction lists
lexicographically, then reactions in their induced order, then species in the
order \(X<Y\); if a network is moved to its canonical orientation, move the
pair with it. A positive answer must return the first such triple and exact
certificates. A negative answer must cover every canonical orbit and every
pair, not merely sampled parameter values.

This scope includes only the six fixed complexes, networks with three or four
reactions, and classical mass-action specializations at positive steady states.
“Identically zero” is algebraic vanishing on the steady-flux variety as defined
above; it is not vanishing at a sample, numerical cancellation, or a statement
about kinetics outside this frozen class.

## Validation and nearest literature

The exact boundary example

\[
0\to X,\quad X\to Y,\quad Y\to0
\]

is retained: \(v=(1,1,1)\), \(\operatorname{rank}S=2\), and, with the two
nonzero generic derivatives denoted \(a,b\),
\(\det(SR_{\rm gen})=ab\). With Gale vector \((1,1,1)^T\), also
\(\det A_{\rm gen}=ab\), and its species rows are
\((-1/a,1/a,0)\) and \((-1/b,0,1/b)\). This checks that the retained class is
nonempty and that the stated row/column convention is coherent; it does not
resolve the census.

Primary source inspected on 2026-09-10: Kuhrs, Telek, and Vassena,
*Layered mixed matrices and reaction networks*, arXiv:2609.05100v1,
<https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05100v1>. Equations (15), (22)--(25), and (45)
fix \(S,S^-,R,A\) and the inverse sensitivity convention. Remark 3.6 says
classical mass action is not parameter-rich and imposes extra algebraic
constraints requiring further analysis. Theorem 3.23 classifies nonzero
entries only in the independent-symbol model. Searches on 2026-09-10 for the
exact census phrase and for equivalent combinations of classical mass action,
parameter-rich kinetics, structural influence, and zero sensitivity found no
primary source that states or settles this exact six-complex census. That
limited search is not a priority or openness claim. Source status is therefore
`new-question` (with wider novelty status still unverified), not
`open-supported`.
