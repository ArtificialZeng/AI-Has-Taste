# Frozen source: positivity of one-sided fractional Gaussian noise predictors

Author-posed original: Mishura, Ralchenko and Schilling, *Analytical and
Computational Problems Related to Fractional Gaussian Noise*, arXiv:2209.02501v1,
printed page 2, Conjecture (A); journal Fractal Fract. 6 (2022), 620.

The same all-dimensions assertion is Conjecture 3, printed page 3, of
Bodnarchuk, Mishura and Ralchenko, *Fractional Gaussian Noise: Projections,
Prediction, Norms*, Fractal Fract. 9 (2025), 428, published 29 June 2025.
https://doi.org/10.3390/fractalfract9070428

Precise transcription of the mathematical assertion (not a newly generated
claim): for every H in (1/2,1) and every integer n>=2, let rho_0=1 and
rho_j=((j+1)^(2H)-2*j^(2H)+abs(j-1)^(2H))/2 for integers j>=1.
Let Gamma_n^k, k=2,...,n, be the unique solution of
sum_{k=2}^n Gamma_n^k rho_{abs(l-k)}=rho_{l-1}, l=2,...,n.
Are all Gamma_n^k strictly positive?

This is the ONE-SIDED finite projection of Delta_1 onto Delta_2,...,Delta_n,
not bilateral conditioning. H=1/2 and H=1 are excluded; the latter has a
degenerate covariance matrix. A bilateral negative coefficient is not a
counterexample to this source. No finite n restriction silently replaces it.
