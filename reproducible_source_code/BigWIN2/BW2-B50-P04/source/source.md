# Immutable source — win-00050-p04

## Original lead

Solve the first untreated fixed-black-square extremal layer for crossword rook placements.

## Precise frozen statement for triage

For every integer `n>=3`, let `M_2(n)` be the maximum of `|RP(G)|` over all `n x n` crossword grids with exactly two black squares, where words and complete non-attacking rook placements are defined exactly as in Section 2 of the primary source. Determine a closed exact formula for `M_2(n)` and classify, under the natural symmetries generated by horizontal/vertical reflection and transposition, every two-black-square configuration attaining it. Boundary values and exceptional small `n` must be stated explicitly.

The proof should split the relative positions of the two black squares, derive exact permanent/counting formulas, and prove the discrete maximum. Exact exhaustive enumeration for at least `3<=n<=12`, with two independent counting implementations and symmetry-orbit checks, is required as regression evidence but cannot replace the all-`n` proof. Any grid exceeding the proposed value or any omitted equality orbit disproves the candidate.
