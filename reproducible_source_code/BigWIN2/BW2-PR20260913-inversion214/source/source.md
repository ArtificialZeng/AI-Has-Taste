# Class 214: generating function and non-algebraicity

Faithful mathematical transcription of the author-posed questions, not a
browser-generated batch. Nathan Britt and Nicholas Beaton, *Completing the
enumeration of inversion sequences avoiding triples of relations*,
arXiv2512.21943v2 (3 August 2026), Section3.5, printed p18.

For n>=0 let I_n consist of integer sequences (e_1,...,e_n) such that
0<=e_i<i for every i and there are no i<j<k with both e_j>=e_k and
e_i>=e_k. The empty sequence is counted once. Equivalently these sequences
avoid the patterns 000,010,100,110,120,210. Define
F(z)=sum_(n>=0) |I_n| z^n in Q[[z]].

Determine F(z) and prove or disprove the source's non-algebraicity
conjecture: for every nonzero P(z,w) in Q[z,w], P(z,F(z)) is not zero.
An explicit coefficient recurrence already exists in equation(3.31),
and the auxiliary functional equation(3.32)-(3.33) is not a new solution.
