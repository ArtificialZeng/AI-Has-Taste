# Primary-source comparison (2026-09-07)

The decisive prior source is William Kasel, *Structural rigidity in the
Erdős–Graham two-set permutation problem*, arXiv:2609.02939v1 (submitted
2026-08-31), <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02939>.  The exact inspected PDF is
copied at `evidence/external/2609.02939.pdf`, SHA-256
`22997afac6b8922d994dbfc8fb111d7ea45321cb49bf6b4a44c71c3a09ad61c9`.

Definition 15 defines `OG(M)`.  Proposition 19 reports finite C4 UNSAT checks
on swept multiples of four but explicitly makes no claim beyond that sweep.
Theorems 22 and 28 prove only a different C3 core for `M=0 (mod 8)`; the text
states that its FLIP proof fails for `M=4 (mod 8)`.  Remark 30 leaves full
`OG(M)` infeasibility conjectural outside the proved residue.  Thus the theorem
in `c4_uniform_proof.md` supplies precisely the source-identified missing
uniform C4 argument on `M=4 (mod 8)`.
