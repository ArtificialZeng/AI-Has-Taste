For every integer m &gt;= 6, the identifying edges of J_m within the class of finite simple maximal planar graphs are exactly pw, wr_0, and wr_1. Under the full automorphism group they form the two orbits {pw} and {wr_0,wr_1}; Aut(J_m) is generated by r_i -&gt; r_{1-i}. Thus there are three identifying edges. Their two unlabelled card types have full-deck multiplicities 1 and 2, respectively. Every remaining edge orbit has the explicit nonisomorphic maximal-planar flip rival specified in evidence/classification-proof.md; the complete edge-orbit count is 3m/2+3 for even m and (3m+7)/2 for odd m.
