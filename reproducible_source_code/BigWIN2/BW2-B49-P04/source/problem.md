# Precise problem reading

## Frozen claim and scope

The immutable lead is `source.md`.  The mathematical task is the following
classification problem, with no assertion in advance that any particular edge
other than the known witness `wp` is identifying.

For every integer \(m\ge 6\), subscripts on \(r_i\) are read modulo \(m\).  Define
the finite simple graph \(J_m\) by
\[
 V(J_m)=\{p,q,w,r_0,\ldots,r_{m-1}\}
\]
and
\[
 E(J_m)=\{r_i r_{i+1}:0\le i<m\}
 \cup\{pr_i,qr_i:0\le i<m\}
 \cup\{wp,wr_0,wr_1\}.
\]
Thus \(pq\notin E(J_m)\), and there are no edges other than those displayed.
Equivalently, start with the bipyramid over the \(m\)-cycle and stack \(w\)
in the facial triangle \(pr_0r_1\).  The graph has \(m+3\) vertices and
\(3m+3=3(m+3)-6\) edges and is a maximal planar graph.

Let \(\mathcal M\) be the class of all finite simple edge-maximal planar graphs,
considered up to graph isomorphism.  Deleting an edge retains both endpoints.
For \(e\in E(J_m)\), the card \(J_m-e\) is unlabelled, and \(e\) is
**identifying** precisely when
\[
 \forall H\in\mathcal M\;\forall f\in E(H),\qquad
 H-f\cong J_m-e\ \Longrightarrow\ H\cong J_m.                 \tag{1}
\]
This expands “occurs as an edge card” and makes clear that the comparison is
with every maximal-planar parent, not only a flip mate found in one embedding.

For each such \(m\), determine:

1. the subset \(I_m\subseteq E(J_m)\) of edges satisfying (1);
2. its partition into orbits for the natural action of the full abstract graph
   automorphism group \(\operatorname{Aut}(J_m)\) on edges;
3. \(|I_m|\), in closed form as a function of \(m\), and, for each unlabelled
   card type \(C\) arising from an identifying edge, its full-deck multiplicity
   \[
      \mu_m(C)=|\{e\in E(J_m):J_m-e\cong C\}|;
   \]
   card isomorphisms between different edge orbits must therefore be checked;
4. for every edge orbit disjoint from \(I_m\), an explicit finite simple
   maximal-planar graph \(H\not\cong J_m\) and edge \(f\in E(H)\) with
   \(H-f\cong J_m-e\) for an orbit representative \(e\).

Identifying status depends only on the unlabelled card type, so if two edges of
\(J_m\) give isomorphic cards, either both are identifying or neither is.
Uniform arguments must cover every \(m\ge6\); parity cases and exceptional
small values cannot be omitted.

## Boundary and normalization checks

The displayed construction is internally consistent.  Its vertex degrees are
\[
 \deg(w)=3,\quad \deg(p)=m+1,\quad \deg(q)=m,\quad
 \deg(r_0)=\deg(r_1)=5,
\]
with every other rim vertex of degree \(4\).  For \(m\ge6\), this already fixes
\(p,q,w\) under every automorphism and fixes the set \(\{r_0,r_1\}\).  The rim
cycle then shows, as a preliminary exact check, that
\(\operatorname{Aut}(J_m)\cong C_2\), generated by
\(r_i\mapsto r_{1-i}\) while fixing \(p,q,w\).  This makes the orbit
classification finite and explicit, but it does not determine identifying
status or card-type coincidences.

The lower bound \(m\ge6\) is part of the frozen target even though the nearest
source defines and treats the one-card witness for \(m\ge4\).  No claim about
\(m=4,5\) is included.

## Literature triage (retrieved 2026-09-07)

The nearest primary source is Sergey Ivanov, *The Class Edge-Reconstruction
Number of a Maximal Planar Graph Is One or Two*, arXiv:2609.02389v1:

- official abstract/version record: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02389>;
- official PDF: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02389>.

The official record gives submission date 2026-09-02.  Direct inspection of
v1 found: Definition and Theorem 2.2 on pp. 2--3 (every finite simple maximal
planar graph is identified by at most two selected edge cards), Proposition
2.4 on p. 3 (the exact maximal-planar-parent test), Lemma 3.1 on p. 3 (the two
parents of a flippable-edge card, including three-connectivity), and Figure 4
with Proposition 5.2 on p. 5 (for every \(m\ge4\), deleting \(wp\) from \(J_m\)
has two maximal-planar completions and they are isomorphic, so this card
identifies \(J_m\)).  Section 6 on p. 6 asks for an intrinsic characterization
of maximal planar graphs for which one card suffices.

The paper proves existence of an identifying edge for each \(J_m\), not the
requested classification of all edges, automorphism orbits, card-type
multiplicities, or explicit rivals for every failed orbit.  Exact-phrase and
equivalent-language web searches for `J_m`/`Jm`, identifying edges, one-card
maximal-planar graphs, and classification of all identifying edges returned
this source but no separate primary result settling the refinement.  This is a
limited literature screen, not a priority or open-status proof.  Accordingly
the source status is **new-question** (a precise proposed refinement, not an
author-declared open problem), with novelty still to be rechecked before any
release claim.
