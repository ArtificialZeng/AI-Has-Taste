# Precise problem: formal qMZV injectivity through weight eight

## Frozen reading

Work over \(\mathbb Q\), with \(q\) a formal variable and all q-series in
\(\mathbb Q[[q]]\).  Put

\[
R_0=\mathbb Q\!\left[\frac q{1-q}\right],\qquad
z_i=\frac{q^i}{(1-q)^i}\quad(i\geq0),
\]

so \((z_i)_{i\geq0}\) is a basis of \(R_0\),
\(R_0^+=R_0\cap q\mathbb Q[[q]]=\bigoplus_{i\geq1}\mathbb Qz_i\), and
\(z_i z_j=z_{i+j}\).  In the free associative algebra
\(H=\mathbb Q\langle x,z_0\rangle\), use the same notation
\(z_i=x^i z_0\), and set

\[
H^0=\mathbb Q1\oplus xHz_0\simeq QS(R_0^+,R_0).
\]

Thus a basis of \(H^0\) consists of the empty word and the *admissible*
words \(z_{s_1}\cdots z_{s_k}\) with \(k\geq1\), \(s_1\geq1\), and
\(s_2,\ldots,s_k\geq0\).  The quasi-shuffle product is determined by

\[
1*u=u*1=u,
\]
\[
(z_a u)*(z_b v)=z_a(u*z_bv)+z_b(z_au*v)+z_{a+b}(u*v).
\]

Define the anti-involution \(\tau:H\to H\) by
\(\tau(uv)=\tau(v)\tau(u)\), \(\tau(x)=z_0\), and
\(\tau(z_0)=x\).  It preserves \(H^0\).  Let

\[
I=(\tau(u)-u:u\in H^0)_*
\]

be the full two-sided (equivalently, since \(*\) is commutative, ordinary)
\(*\)-ideal generated by all duality differences, and put
\(\mathcal Z_*^{\mathrm f}=H_*^0/I\).  It is not enough to quotient by the
bare linear span of the generators.

For a basis word define

\[
Z(1)=1,\qquad
Z(z_{s_1}\cdots z_{s_k})=
\sum_{n_1>\cdots>n_k>0}
\prod_{j=1}^k\left(\frac{q^{n_j}}{1-q^{n_j}}\right)^{s_j}.
\]

The condition \(s_1\geq1\) makes every formal coefficient well-defined.
Theorem 3.5 of Mozgovoy proves \(Z\circ\tau=Z\), and the quasi-shuffle
identity makes \(Z\) an algebra map.  Hence \(I\subseteq\ker Z\) and \(Z\)
descends to the canonical surjection
\(\bar Z:\mathcal Z_*^{\mathrm f}\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal Z_{q,0}\).

Give letters and words weights

\[
\operatorname{wt}(z_s)=\max(s,1),\qquad
\operatorname{wt}(z_{s_1}\cdots z_{s_k})=
\sum_j\max(s_j,1),
\]

with \(\operatorname{wt}(1)=0\).  Let \(W_nH^0\) be the span of words of
weight at most \(n\), and give the quotient the induced filtration
\(W_n\mathcal Z_*^{\mathrm f}=(W_nH^0+I)/I\).

## Quantified assertion

Prove or disprove

\[
\ker\!\left(\bar Z\big|_{W_8\mathcal Z_*^{\mathrm f}}\right)=0.
\]

Equivalently, prove

\[
\forall a\in W_8H^0,\quad Z(a)=0\Longrightarrow a\in I,
\]

or find one exact \(a\in W_8H^0\) for which \(Z(a)=0\) but \(a\notin I\).
This includes weights 0 through 8, permits zero indices after the first
index, and concerns formal equality of every q-coefficient.  It does not ask
for Conjecture 3.6 in weights above 8.  Formula (44), being conditional on
two conjectures, is not an available dimension theorem.

As a finite-size check, \(W_8H^0\) has exactly 1597 word-basis elements
(including \(1\)); the exact-weight counts for weights 1 through 8 are
\(1,3,8,21,55,144,377,987\).  This is only an enumeration, not evidence for
injectivity.

## Literature status and nearest prior result (checked 2026-09-09)

- S. Mozgovoy, *Multiple zeta values in lambda-rings*,
  [arXiv:2609.04424v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04424v1), Section 3.2,
  equations (42)--(44), Theorem 3.5 and Conjecture 3.6 (HTML lines
  619--655).  It proves duality lies in the kernel and explicitly leaves the
  assertion that the induced map is an isomorphism as a conjecture.
- B. Brindle, *On the structure of Multiple q-Zeta Values*,
  [arXiv:2511.07302v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.07302), pp. 1--2.  With
  \(u_i=z_i\), its admissible stuffle algebra, weight, involution, quotient
  \(\mathcal Z_q^{\mathrm f}\), and analytic q-series are the same objects
  (the displayed binomial factors result from summing the intervening
  zero-index letters).  Its Conjecture 1 says all analytic relations are
  generated by stuffle and duality; the paper proves other structural
  subspace statements, not the present analytic injectivity through weight
  8.
- Y. Takeyama, *The Algebra of a q-Analogue of Multiple Harmonic Series*,
  [arXiv:1306.6164v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1306.6164), p. 2 and pp. 14--15.
  It reports a computer experiment in which double-shuffle and resummation
  duality account for all relations among modified qMZVs only through
  weight 7; its final table stops at \(d=7\).  This is the nearest finite
  computational precedent, but it neither covers weight 8 nor by itself
  supplies an exact certificate for the present quotient.

No exact weight-8 settlement was found in these inspected primary sources.
That scoped search is support for advancing the computation, not a priority
or exhaustive-literature claim.

## Proposed delta and verification route

Nearest prior range: Takeyama's reported computation through weight 7.
Proposed delta: an exact certificate for the same kernel-generation question
on the precisely defined filtered space through weight 8.  Verification
route: enumerate \(W_8H^0\); compute \(I\cap W_8H^0\) using all required
quasi-shuffle multiples; row-reduce over \(\mathbb Q\); choose an exact basis
of the quotient; and expand its images in \(\mathbb Q[[q]]\) until a finite
rational coefficient matrix has full column rank.  Full column rank proves
injectivity; finite rank deficiency alone proves nothing.
