## Original target, mathematical paraphrase

For every finite simple tree T with n>=2 vertices, let s_k(T) be the number
of nonempty connected induced vertex subsets of size k and put
Phi_T(z)=sum_{k=1}^n s_k(T) z^k. Prove or disprove that every complex zero z
of Phi_T satisfies

    1/2 <= |z+1/2| <= 1/2+(n-1)^(1/(n-1)).

Both inequalities and all n>=2 belong to the original target. The zero z=0
is included and attains the inner boundary. Do not silently replace this by
a nonzero-root, fixed-order, tree-subclass, real-root or origin-centred claim.
Any such useful partial result must retain the general original as unresolved.
