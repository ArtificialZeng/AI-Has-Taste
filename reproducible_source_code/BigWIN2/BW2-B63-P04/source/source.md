# Frozen source — win-00063-p04

For every unlabeled tree `T` on exactly 21 vertices, form its independence polynomial

`I(T,z)=sum_{k>=0} i_k(T) z^k`,

where `i_k(T)` is the number of independent `k`-vertex sets. Decide whether every such polynomial is stable, meaning that all of its complex zeros satisfy `Re(z)<=0`.

If the answer is no, classify all unstable isomorphism classes and give an exact Routh-Hurwitz or real-algebraic certificate for at least one right-half-plane zero of each class. If the answer is yes, give a complete unlabeled-tree census and exact stability certificates. The source reports computation only through 20 vertices and proves all spiders stable.

Primary source: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04694>, Theorem 2 and Theorem 4; PDF SHA-256 `fb1940a98c1cc1ec1e5ba479871431244090771b2b6ce3fa7c72dc888c6c4d82`.
