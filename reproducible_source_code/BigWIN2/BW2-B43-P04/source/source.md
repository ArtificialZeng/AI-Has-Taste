# Frozen source — win-00043-p04

Let `M_d(Delta)` be the maximum B-chromatic index `q_B(G)` over all finite simple `d`-degenerate graphs `G` with maximum degree at most `Delta` and containing no copy of `K_{d,Delta}`, exactly as in Hu–Kong–Wang, arXiv:2609.02845v1, Section 4. Determine the exact value

> `M_3(4)`.

The source proves `3Delta-3<=M_3(Delta)<=3Delta-2`, hence `M_3(4)` is either 9 or 10, and explicitly asks which value occurs. A value 10 needs a concrete legal graph plus an exact proof that nine B-colors are impossible and ten suffice. A value 9 needs a complete proof for the whole quantified graph class; bounded enumeration is acceptable only with independently checkable coverage and unsatisfiability certificates.
