# Frozen source statement: win-00054-p05

Determine the exact adjacent-vertex-distinguishing total chromatic number
\[
\chi''_a(C_3\square C_3).
\]
A total coloring assigns colors to all vertices and edges, with adjacent vertices, adjacent edges, and incident vertex-edge pairs differently colored. For each vertex `u`, let `C(u)` be the set containing its vertex color and the colors on its incident edges; for every edge `uv`, require `C(u) != C(v)`. A complete resolution must provide a machine-checkable coloring at the optimum and a proof or exact UNSAT certificate for every smaller feasible color count.

Primary source: J. Geetha and K. Somasundaram, *Adjacent vertex distinguishing total chromatic number of graph products*, arXiv:2609.03411v1, Definitions in the abstract/Introduction and Section 1.2. The paper proves several product upper bounds but does not state the exact value for this non-bipartite Cartesian product. Nearby registry item win-00044-p09 concerns the much broader finite frontier for all maximum-degree-five graphs, not this exact graph.
