# win-00080-p02 — The omitted cubic-root ozone group of U_q^+(B_2)

## Immutable target

Let k be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero and q in k* a primitive third root of unity. Let A=U_q^+(B_2) be generated by e1,e2 with the source's two quantum Serre relations:

e1^2 e2 - (q^2+q^(-2)) e1 e2 e1 + e2 e1^2 = 0,

e2^3 e1 - (q^2+1+q^(-2)) e2^2 e1 e2
+ (q^2+1+q^(-2)) e2 e1 e2^2 - e1 e2^3 = 0.

Determine Oz(A)=Aut_{Z(A)-alg}(A), the automorphisms of A fixing its center pointwise. In particular, prove that it is trivial or explicitly verify a nonidentity element.

A proof may not substitute m=3 into a theorem stated only for m>=5. It must verify the center description and all automorphism-classification hypotheses used at the cubic root. A counterexample must define an invertible relation-preserving automorphism and prove that it fixes all of Z(A) pointwise.

## Provenance and nearest theorem

James Gómez and Helbert Venegas, *The ozone group of U_q^+(B_2)*,
arXiv:2609.04919v1. Section 3.2 starts on PDF p. 5 by assuming that q is a
primitive m-th root with m>=5. Proposition 3.3 (pp. 5--6) records central
powers, Lemma 3.5 (p. 6) imports the full center description, Proposition 3.6
(pp. 6--7) forces the diagonal automorphism form, and Theorem 3.7 (pp. 7--8)
then gives Oz(A) isomorphic to mu_gcd(ell,2), ell=ord(q^2). For m=3,
ell=3, so the formula predicts a trivial group, but that parameter is outside
the theorem's stated range. The explicit denominators q^2-1 and q^4-1 remain
nonzero at m=3; this is only a lead, not permission to reuse the center theorem.
