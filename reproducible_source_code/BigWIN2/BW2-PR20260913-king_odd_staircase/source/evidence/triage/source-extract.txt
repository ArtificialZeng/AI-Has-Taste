Definition 2.1 (Partition Families).



Given positive integers r,c, and k, the rectangle, staircase, hook, and generalized staircase partitions are defined respectively as




\displaystyle[diagram]_{r,c}
\displaystyle=\llbracket c^{r}\rrbracket,
\displaystyle[diagram]^{k}
\displaystyle=\llbracket k,k-1,k-2,\ldots,1\rrbracket,



\displaystyle[diagram]_{r,c}
\displaystyle=\llbracket c,1^{r-1}\rrbracket,
\displaystyle[diagram]^{k}_{r,c}
\displaystyle=\llbracket(kc)^{r},((k-1)c)^{r},\ldots,c^{r}\rrbracket.







See Figure 2 for examples. These definitions imply [diagram]^{k}_{1,1}=[diagram]^{k}
and
[diagram]^{1}_{r,c}=[diagram]_{r,c}.

The parts of generalized staircases are easily computed from r,c, and k.

Definition 3.1.



We study the following impartial chess games.




• 



Downright is played on the moveset \mathcal{D}=\{(0,1),(1,0)\}.

• 



Pawn is played on the moveset \sympawn=\{(0,1),(1,1)\}.

• 



Knight is played on the moveset \symknight=\{(1,2),(2,1)\}.

• 



Bishop is played on the moveset \symbishop=(1,1)^{+}.

• 



King is played on the moveset \symking=\{(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)\}.

• 



Rook is played on the moveset \symrook=(1,0)^{+}\cup(0,1)^{+}.

• 



Queen is played on the moveset \symqueen=(1,1)^{+}\cup(1,0)^{+}\cup(1,1)^{+}.

Theorem 3.8 (Berlekamp).



Let r,c be positive integers. Then




\mathbb{SG}_{\symking}\left([diagram]_{r,c}\right)=\begin{cases}0&\text{if $c$ and $r$ are odd}\\
2&\text{if $c$ and $r$ are even}\\
1&\text{if $c+r$ is odd and $\min(c,r)$ is odd}\\
3&\text{if $c+r$ is odd and $\min(c,r)$ is even}\end{cases}

Lemma 3.13.



Let k\in\mathbb{Z}^{\geq 0}. Then \mathbb{SG}_{\symking}([diagram]^{k})=(k-1)\bmod 3.

Lemma 3.14.



Let r be even. Then \mathbb{SG}_{\symking}([diagram]^{k}_{r,r})=\begin{cases}2&\text{if $k=1$,}\\
1&\text{otherwise.}\end{cases}

Conjecture 5.1.



Let r be odd and k\geq 1. Then




\mathbb{SG}_{\symking}([diagram]^{k}_{r,r})=\begin{cases}0&\text{$k\bmod(r+2)$ is odd}\\
1&\text{$k\bmod(r+2)$ is even and not 0}\\
2&\text{$k\bmod(r+2)$ is 0}.\end{cases}
