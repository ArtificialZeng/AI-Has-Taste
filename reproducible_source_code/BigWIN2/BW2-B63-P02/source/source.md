# Frozen source — win-00063-p02

For every integer `r>=2`, let `J(4r+2,2r,r)` be the generalized Johnson graph whose vertices are the `2r`-subsets of `[4r+2]`, with two distinct vertices adjacent exactly when their intersection has size `r`.

Determine the least integer `r0` such that, for every `r>=r0`, the Eberlein eigenvalue indexed by `j=2`, denoted `E_r(2)` in arXiv:2609.04677v1, is the smallest adjacency eigenvalue of `J(4r+2,2r,r)`. Classify every exception `2<=r<r0` by giving the true minimizing index or indices. Supply exact integer/rational comparisons and a proof covering the infinite tail.

Primary source: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04677>, Proposition 12 and Theorem 13; PDF SHA-256 `de3b37a3c87eeeef297382da835922996e804e6b2ab44d1a2e763cc4fa9881f3`.
