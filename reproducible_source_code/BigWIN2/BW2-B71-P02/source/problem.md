# Precise problem statement

## Frozen objects and conventions

Work over the complex scalars. Let \(X\) be the particular Motakis realisation over
\(S^4\) selected in the proof of Theorem 5.1 (Theorem A) of Horváth--Kania,
arXiv:2609.05362v1: the auxiliary metric is chosen so that the entries of the
Klaja--Ransford functions \(a,b\), and the function used to exponentiate
\(d=I_2-2ba\), have the prescribed diagonal lifts. Let

\[
E=X\oplus X,\qquad S=\widehat a,\qquad T=\widehat b\in\mathcal B(E)
\]

be exactly the space and operators constructed there. These are fixed objects;
there is no quantification over other Motakis realisations, other choices of
lifts, or arbitrary operators.

For a complex unital Banach algebra \(A\), let

\[
\operatorname{Inv}(A)=\{u\in A:u\text{ is invertible}\},
\]

let \(\operatorname{Inv}_0(A)\) be the norm-connected (equivalently,
norm-path-connected) component of \(1_A\) in \(\operatorname{Inv}(A)\), and set

\[
\mathfrak I(A)=\operatorname{Inv}(A)/\operatorname{Inv}_0(A).
\]

The identity component is a normal subgroup, so this is the group of connected
components. The first stabilisation homomorphism is

\[
s_A:\mathfrak I(A)\longrightarrow\mathfrak I(M_2(A)),\qquad
s_A([u])=\left[\begin{pmatrix}u&0\\0&1_A\end{pmatrix}\right].
\]

Thus \([u]\in\ker s_A\) precisely when
\(\operatorname{diag}(u,1_A)\in\operatorname{Inv}_0(M_2(A))\). The order of
\([u]\) is the least \(n\geq 1\) for which \([u]^n=[1_A]\), if such an \(n\)
exists, and is infinite otherwise.

## Question

Determine, with equality rather than only an inclusion,

\[
\ker\!\left(s_{\mathcal B(E)}:\mathfrak I(\mathcal B(E))
\longrightarrow\mathfrak I(M_2(\mathcal B(E)))\right),
\]

and determine the order in \(\mathfrak I(\mathcal B(E))\) of the fixed class
\([I_E-2ST]\).

## Established source facts and proposed delta

Theorem 5.1 and Proposition 7.3 of arXiv:2609.05362v1 imply that
\([I_E-2ST]\neq[1_E]\) and \([I_E-2ST]\in\ker s_{\mathcal B(E)}\). Under the
Calkin identification

\[
\mathcal Q(E)\cong C(S^4,M_2(\mathbb C)),
\]

its image is the nonzero element of
\(\pi_4(\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb C))\cong\mathbb Z/2\). Question 9.1 of
that version explicitly says that neither the full upstairs kernel nor the
upstairs order is known.

The proposed delta is to prove, rather than assume, that the Calkin quotient is
injective on component groups for these basis-bearing spaces, and then combine
that lemma with the two Calkin identifications and the unstable homotopy groups
to obtain

\[
\mathfrak I(\mathcal B(E))\cong\mathbb Z/2,\qquad
\mathfrak I(M_2(\mathcal B(E)))=1.
\]

If every step survives proof audit, this would force the requested kernel to be
all of \(\mathfrak I(\mathcal B(E))\), generated by \([I_E-2ST]\), and would
give that class order two. This paragraph is a target, not an established
result of the present triage.

## Scope boundary

No claim is made about arbitrary Banach spaces, arbitrary Motakis realisations,
real scalars, higher spheres, arbitrary quotient extensions, or stabilization
kernels beyond the fixed source construction. Any general Calkin-component
lemma used in a proof must state its own hypotheses separately.
