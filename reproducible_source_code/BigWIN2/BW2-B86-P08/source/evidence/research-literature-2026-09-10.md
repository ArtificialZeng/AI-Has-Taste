# Research-pass literature verification (2026-09-10)

Provenance: research job `win-00086-p08-research-f04f813e430b`.

The project contained no file matching `literature/*handoff*.md` and no local
copy of the primary paper.  The current arXiv abstract and HTML for George M.
Georgiou, *Congruent Triangular Faces, Reflections Allowed: Universal
Realization and the Minimum Face Count in Problem B22*, arXiv:2608.01109, were
therefore inspected live at

- <https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01109>
- <https://arxiv.org/html/2608.01109>

The abstract page's submission history showed only v1, submitted 2026-08-02.
The HTML text states in Section 1 that the paper uses ordinary congruence,
including orientation-reversing isometries.  Remark 3 characterizes handedness
by the cyclic order of the three distinct side lengths read with respect to
the outward normal and says that its eight facets split into four direct and
four mirror-image copies.  This matches the frozen direct-copy convention for
either of the two possible reference cyclic orders.  Lemma 5 gives the
disphenoid coordinate squares, including
`(a^2+b^2-c^2)/8`.  The concluding discussion says that the
orientation-preserving problem remains open only for right and obtuse scalene
triangles.

Bounded web searches used the exact arXiv title and combinations of
"orientation-preserving", "right or obtuse scalene", "congruent triangular
faces", "direct copies", and the oriented triangulation/edge-labeling
formulation.  They found Georgiou's paper and older adjacent work, but no later
primary paper resolving this exact remainder.  This is a dated negative
screen, not a proof of priority or an exhaustive citation search.

The mathematical proof submitted in this project does not depend on treating
the cited Lemma 5 as a black box: it independently derives the necessary
disphenoid coordinates and the strict acuteness inequality.  Thus the absence
of a local paper file does not leave an external theorem as an unsupported
decisive step.
