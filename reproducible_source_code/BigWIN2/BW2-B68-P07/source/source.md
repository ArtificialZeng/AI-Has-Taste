## Definition boundary

Follow the literal independent-row definition in the newer source:

```
G_alpha = <sigma_(a_1)> times ... times <sigma_(a_k)>.
```

Thus repeated equal rows produce independent cyclic factors. The predecessor's
Definition 3.3 instead uses one diagonal cyclic factor for each distinct part size,
acting simultaneously on all rows of that size. These are different subgroups when
parts repeat; no result may be transferred between them without proof.

## Frozen assertion

In degree four, put

```
H = G_22 = <(12),(34)>,
L = G_4  = <(1234)>.
```

Disprove the assertion that the molecular-species product `K_22 times K_4` has a
nonnegative integer expansion in the literal independent-row basis
`{K_lambda : lambda partitions 4}`. Also decide whether degree four is the first
degree where such closure fails.

## Candidate certificate to recompute independently

The intake calculation proposes two `H`--`L` double cosets represented by `e` and
`pi=(23)`. It proposes

```
H intersection L = {e},
H intersection pi L pi^(-1) = <(12)(34)>,
```

with double-coset sizes 16 and 8, exhausting `S_4`. The second intersection is not
conjugate to an independent-row `G_lambda`: the only order-two candidate in degree
four, `G_211`, is generated by a transposition rather than a double transposition.
Consequently the proposed species and cycle-index decompositions are

```
K_22 times K_4  isomorphic to  K_(1,1,1,1) plus C_22,
K_22 star K_4 = 2 K_22 - 2 K_211 + 2 K_(1,1,1,1).
```

If correct, the coefficient `-2` proves failure of nonnegative K-basis closure.
An intake enumeration found no obstruction through degree three.

## Required verification and claim boundary

Reconstruct every subgroup and double coset in `S_4` from explicit permutations;
verify the standard double-coset product formula; derive both displayed identities
from exact cycle indices; prove linear independence in the relevant degree before
interpreting the negative coefficient; and exhaust every partition pair through
degree three under the same literal definition. Preserve scripts, canonical tables,
hashes, and an independent replay.

The frozen claim concerns nonnegative K-basis closure, not the existence of a
closed formula over a larger molecular-species basis. A counterexample to closure
does not by itself answer every aspect of Open Problem 5.1.
