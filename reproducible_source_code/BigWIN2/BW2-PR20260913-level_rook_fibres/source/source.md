# Frozen source: fibres of the m-level transpose

Primary original: Barrese, Loehr, Remmel and Sagan, *Bijections on m-level
Rook Placements*, arXiv1508.05944v1, Section6.2 printed21. The journal
version is EJC57(2016),13–35, Section6.2 printed33,
DOI10.1016/j.ejc.2016.03.005. The source asks, for singleton C, about
“counting the number of Ferrers boards B with l(B) = C”.

https://arxiv.org/html/1508.05944v1

This is the author's explicitly posed preimage-counting subquestion, not a
newly invented restriction. The broader count of a whole m-level rook
equivalence class is context and is NOT silently claimed solved by a fibre
result. The exact full primary text is preserved as
literature/blrs-bijections-1508.05944v1.pdf with SHA256
895f4c66c64a0092b8990660f9a88249c203677fae28bd10e407b20600362f22.

Interpretation to validate: for every integer m>=1 and every singleton
Ferrers shape C, determine the cardinality of all Ferrers shapes B with
l_m(B)=C. Shapes identify leading zero columns; column heights otherwise
form a finite weakly increasing positive sequence. The empty shape is allowed.
Definitions, normalization provenance, nearest known results and candidate
quality thresholds are separate in problem.md and literature/outer-intake-handoff.md.
This source is immutable. Neither the bounded literature search nor finite
calibration is mathematical acceptance or a worldwide novelty guarantee.
