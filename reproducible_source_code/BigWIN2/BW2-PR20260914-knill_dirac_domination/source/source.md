# Knill: connection–Dirac spectral-sum domination

Source: Oliver Knill, Remarks about Connection and Dirac matrices, arXiv:2601.18071v2 (29 July 2026), Conjecture 1, Section 5.3, printed page 9 (PDF page index 8).
https://arxiv.org/html/2601.18071v2
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.18071v2

Verbatim numbered claim (notation retained):
> If L is the connection matrix and D the Dirac matrix, then L−D satisfies S_k(L)≥S_k(D).

Source-defined setting: G is a finite abstract simplicial complex of nonempty faces, closed under taking nonempty subsets; n=|G|. Its connection matrix has L_xy=1 when x∩y is nonempty and zero otherwise. D=d+d^T is the oriented face-incidence Dirac matrix, d_xy equal to the codimension-one incidence sign when y⊂x, and zero otherwise. For any real symmetric matrix A, S_k(A) is the sum of its k largest eigenvalues, with multiplicity.

Frozen target: for every such G and every integer 1≤k≤n, decide S_k(L_G)≥S_k(D_G). The empty complex has no admissible k. Arbitrary dimensions and non-flag complexes are included.

Interpretive boundary: retain the source's awkward “L−D satisfies” wording above, but investigate its explicitly displayed comparison S_k(L)≥S_k(D), not positive semidefiniteness of L−D or S_k(L−D)≥0. The separate inverse-matrix question is not this original target. Source status and dedup qualifications belong in the intake dossier; no solution is claimed here.
