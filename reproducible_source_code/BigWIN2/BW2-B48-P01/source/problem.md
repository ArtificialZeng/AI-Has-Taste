## Objects and definitions

Write \(S_3\) for the permutations of \(\{1,2,3\}\), and for
\(\sigma\in S_3\) write \(P_\sigma\) for the permutation matrix with
\((P_\sigma)_{i,\sigma(i)}=1\). Then
\[
\mathcal B_3=\operatorname{conv}\{P_\sigma:\sigma\in S_3\}
=\{X\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}^{3\times3}:X\mathbf1=\mathbf1,
X^T\mathbf1=\mathbf1\}.
\]
It is a four-dimensional polytope in its affine hull and has exactly the six
listed permutation vertices.

Throughout, a face \(G\) means a **nonempty proper face** of \(\mathcal B_3\).
Thus vertices, edges, two-faces, and facets are all included; the empty face
and \(\mathcal B_3\) itself are excluded. Put
\[
V(G)=\operatorname{vert}(\mathcal B_3)\cap G,
\qquad
\mathcal B_3[G]=\operatorname{conv}
 (\operatorname{vert}(\mathcal B_3)\setminus V(G)).
\]
Because \(G\) is proper, the complement vertex set is nonempty. With
\(\|X\|_F^2=\sum_{i,j}x_{ij}^2\), define
\[
d(G)=\min_{X\in G,\,Y\in\mathcal B_3[G]}\|X-Y\|_F,
\qquad
\Phi(\mathcal B_3)=\min_{\varnothing\ne G\subsetneq\mathcal B_3
\atop G\text{ a face}}d(G).
\]

Let \(H\) be the 72-element action generated by independent row and column
permutations and transposition; concretely its elements act as
\(X\mapsto RXP^T\) or \(X\mapsto RX^TP^T\), where \(R,P\) are \(3\times3\)
permutation matrices. “Up to symmetry” means equivalence under this specified
group \(H\) only. No assertion that \(H\) is the entire affine-isometry or
combinatorial automorphism group is needed.

## Exact target and quantifiers

The task is to supply both:

1. an exact algebraic value for \(\Phi(\mathcal B_3)\), with proof that every
   nonempty proper face has distance at least that value and with an equality
   witness; and
2. the complete set of \(H\)-orbits of faces \(G\) satisfying
   \(d(G)=\Phi(\mathcal B_3)\), including proof that no other orbit minimizes.

A decimal value, a computation restricted to facets, or a single minimizing
face is strictly insufficient.

## Intake checks and finite scope

The exact combinatorial check in `evidence/triage_face_enumeration.py` starts
from all six permutation vertices, closes vertex subsets under allowed matrix
supports, and quotients by the specified action. Its replayed output in
`evidence/triage_face_enumeration.txt` gives 48 nonempty proper faces:
\[
(f_0,f_1,f_2,f_3)=(6,15,18,9),
\]
and five \(H\)-orbits: one vertex orbit, two edge orbits, one two-face orbit,
and one facet orbit, with orbit sizes \(6;(6,9);18;9\). This is an exact
integer/set computation and establishes that the proposed search is small; it
does not compute any facial distance.

For each representative one may compute
\(\delta(G)=\operatorname{dist}(\operatorname{aff}G,\mathcal B_3[G])\)
by an exact convex quadratic program. A necessary certificate caution is that
\(\delta(G)=d(G)\) is **not automatic for each individual face**. Lemma 3.3 of
Peña--Wirth justifies minimizing the affine-hull distances globally and says
that a globally minimizing affine closest point lies in its selected face.
For equality-orbit classification, containment of the affine closest point in
\(G\) (or a direct face-to-hull distance certificate) must be checked for each
candidate orbit attaining the smallest \(\delta\).
