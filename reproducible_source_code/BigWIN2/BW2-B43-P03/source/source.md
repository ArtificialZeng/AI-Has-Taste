# Frozen source — win-00043-p03

Prove or disprove the coefficientwise inequalities

> `c_{6,(0,1)}(q)>=0` and `c_{6,(0,3)}(q)>=0`.

The functions and normalization are exactly those in Rostam, arXiv:2609.03738v1. The source's Conjecture 3.10 is known there for odd `e` and for `e=4`; for `e=6` it states that the conjecture is equivalent to coefficientwise positivity of these two series, which is supported only by computer calculation. Preserve both series and all coefficients. A negative coefficient is a complete disproof of the corresponding assertion; a proof needs a valid identity, injection, involution, or other all-orders sign argument. Finite expansion is evidence only.
