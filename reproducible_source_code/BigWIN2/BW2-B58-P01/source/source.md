## Problem

Use the definitions printed in Z.-W. Sun, arXiv:2609.04176v1, equations (6.2)--(6.9). For `0<=v<1`, let

```text
alpha(v)={20v}, beta(v)={41v}, gamma(v)={40v},
z(y)=(1/2-y) mod 1,
h_v(y)=2 floor(20v)-floor(41v)
       +2 1[z(y)<alpha(v)]-1[z(y)<v]+1[y>beta(v)].
```

For `s>=0`, let `K_v(s)` be the integral of the lowest `s` units of mass in the continuum multiset

```text
{ h_v(y)+2r : 0<=y<1, r=0,1,2,... },
```

where Lebesgue measure is used in `y` and ties may be chosen arbitrarily because they have equal cost.

Prove or disprove the universally quantified identity

```text
K_v(n+v) = n^2 + K_v(1+v) - 1
```

for every integer `n>=1` and every real `v in [0,1)`.

If false, supply an exact rational `v`, an integer `n`, the complete constant-interval decomposition of `h_v`, and exact rational cumulative masses and costs proving failure. Identify precisely which later displayed claims in the source depend on this identity; do not assert that a failed local lemma proves Catalan's constant rational or that no repaired irrationality proof exists. If true, give a complete proof covering all breakpoints and justify the generalized-quantile periodicity rather than citing an unprovided finite verification.
