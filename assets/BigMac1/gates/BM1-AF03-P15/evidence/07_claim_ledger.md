## Gate 1 boundary decision

No correction to the central finite target is required. The audited wording is:

> Determine, by an independently verifiable exact certificate, whether the
> full absolute order \(\operatorname{Abs}(D_9)\) admits a normalized flow with
> unit vertex weights. This is the first case beyond Gaetz–Gao's published
> computer-verification report \(n\le8\) for which the dated public search
> found no resolution.

Two qualifications are required in all later writing:

1. “First unverified” means first beyond the *published reported range* and
   within this documented public search; it does not rule out unpublished or
   unindexed work.
2. The desired endpoint is normalized-flow feasibility. Strong Sperner alone,
   or feasibility on a noncrossing interval, does not settle it.
