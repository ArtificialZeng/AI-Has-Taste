## Endpoint and hypotheses

The audited endpoint is precisely the finite specialization (n=5) of
Lentfer's Conjecture 3.2: over a characteristic-zero field, the residue
classes of the 1920 monomials in (B_5^{(1,2)}) form a basis of
(R_5^{(1,2)}).  `problem/source_statement.md` preserves the supplied
problem, and `problem/formal_statement.md` fixes the coefficient field,
supercommutative signs, diagonal action, invariant ideal, path condition,
candidate exponents, and finite endpoint.  Scalar extension from
(mathbb Q) is used only after the rational rank and dimension statements
are proved.

## Human structural proof

`proof/generator_lemma.md` proves, for every (n\geq1), that the full Hilbert
ideal generated by all positive invariants is generated by the (4n)
polarized power sums

\[
p_{r,1}\ (1\leq r\leq n),\qquad
p_{r,U}\ (0\leq r<n,\ U\in\{\theta,\xi,\theta\xi\}).
\]

The proof uses Reynolds spanning and an induction on kernel partitions of
slot maps, followed by Newton identities and the characteristic polynomial.
It explicitly permits a Reynolds image to be zero when a sign-reversing odd
stabilizer is present.  This edge case was independently tested by the exact
identity (R(\theta_1\theta_2)=0).  Full (n=3) Coxeter relations for the
signed action were also checked.  No computed dimension or conjectural
Hilbert series enters the generator proof.
