# Rendered-page visual audit

The final five-page PDF was rendered at 150 dpi, and every page was inspected
at the original 1275 by 1651 pixel render resolution.

- Page 1: title, author block, abstract, context, displays, citation, and footer
  are fully visible; no clipping, overlap, or illegible symbols.
- Page 2: the four-branch theorem display, citations, conditional-law formulas,
  constraints, equation labels, and footer are fully visible and aligned.
- Page 3: the losslessness and symmetry proofs, dual program, section heading,
  exact-certificate table, proof boxes, and footer are fully visible.
- Page 4: the dual lines, certificate verification, bullet list, interpolation
  argument, modulus comparison, radical endpoint, and footer are fully visible.
- Page 5: the continued radical display, scope and assistance section, four
  ancillary filenames, all three bibliography entries, and footer are fully
  visible.

Mathematical fractions, radicals, subscripts, superscripts, set notation,
blackboard-bold probability symbols, accented author names, and citation
labels render consistently.  There are no figures or graphical data series.
The single certificate table has clear rules, spacing, and legible cells.
