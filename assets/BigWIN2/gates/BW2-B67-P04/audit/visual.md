# Rendered-page visual audit

All five pages of `manuscript/main.pdf` were rendered independently to PNG at
150 dpi with `pdftoppm` and inspected at original image resolution after the
table-wrapper removal and clean rebuild.

Pages 1--5 were seen. Page 1 has a legible title, author block, abstract,
displayed word formulas, opening definition, citations, and theorem. Pages 2
and 3 contain the finite mex certificate and all tables; table rules, indices,
triples, equations, and section transitions remain inside the margins with no
collisions. Page 4 contains the minimality proof, full-shape completion,
disclosure, and the first reference; line breaks and citation markers are
legible. Page 5 contains the remaining references with ample margin.

No page shows clipping, overlap, missing glyphs, broken formulas, illegible
type, truncated tables, unintended blank content, or visual distortion. A
layout-preserving text extraction also contains no unresolved `??` markers.
