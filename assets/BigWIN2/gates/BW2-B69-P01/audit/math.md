This also independently validates the compression used in the supplied

one, balance forces the numerator and denominator to have the same number of
factors, and the omitted third-column exponent in each row is then forced.

For a fixed gamma, all coefficients of `N_gamma` and `D_gamma` on their
supports are positive integers.  Therefore support containment is equivalent
to existence of a positive constant: one may take the maximum of the finitely
many numerator/denominator coefficient ratios.  No claim that `C=1` works for
the non-ray generators is needed.

Finally, all Hilbert generators have the same weak coordinate signs.  Their
sums therefore cause no numerator/denominator cancellation, and

```text
N_(gamma+eta) = N_gamma N_eta,
D_(gamma+eta) = D_gamma D_eta.
```

Support containment is preserved by multiplication.  This proves the claim
for every nonzero semigroup point.  The zero vector is the empty sum and has
`N_0=D_0=1`, so it is covered as well.  Weak inequalities also ensure that
the same argument covers every chamber boundary face.
