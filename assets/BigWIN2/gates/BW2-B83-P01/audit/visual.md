every page was opened at original image detail.  Page 1 has a centered title,
complete author block, readable abstract, theorem, and citations.  Page 2 has
clear displayed mathematics and no margin collision.  Page 3 contains the full
degree table, graphicality discussion, and the start of the local lemma without
clipping.  Page 4 cleanly continues the lemma and displays equations (4)--(9).

line breaks.  Page 6 contains all six references with legible accents, DOI text,
and no overflow.

No page has clipped content, overlapping text, illegible symbols, a distorted
table, a missing glyph, or an unexpected blank region.  Page 4 begins with a
normal cross-page continuation of Lemma 4; no content is lost.  The displayed
mathematical symbols, equation numbers, table rules, citations, page numbers, and
PDF metadata are visually consistent with the source.
