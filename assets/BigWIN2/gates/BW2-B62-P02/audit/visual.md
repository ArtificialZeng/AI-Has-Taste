# Rendered-page visual audit

The final PDF was rendered at 144 dpi to five 1224 by 1584 PNG pages. Every
page was inspected individually at original resolution, with the contact sheet
used only as an overview.

| Page | Content inspected | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | title, author block, abstract, definitions, main theorem | clear; no clipping, collision, or missing glyph |
| 2 | encoding, formulas, orbit argument, census table | clear; formulas and table rules legible and within margins |
| 3 | unreduced verifier, witness, margins, first part of longtable | clear; page break and table continuation are valid |
| 4 | continued longtable, disclosure, first bibliography entry | clear; continuation header, rows, and URLs legible |
| 5 | remaining two bibliography entries | clear; deliberately sparse but neither blank nor malformed |

Fonts and mathematical symbols render consistently. Page numbers, equation and
table references, URLs, and the longtable continuation are legible. There are
no figures or charts requiring value-by-value source comparison. No overlap,
truncation, margin overflow, broken hyperlink text, or unsupported strengthening
of the accepted theorem was observed.
