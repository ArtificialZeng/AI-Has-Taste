# Rendered-page visual audit

**Verdict:** ACCEPT  
**PDF:** `manuscript/main.pdf`  
**Pages reviewed:** 1, 2, 3, 4

Every page was rendered at 170 dpi with Poppler and inspected as an image.
The inspected renderings are `audit/rendered/page-1.png` through
`audit/rendered/page-4.png`.

- Page 1: title, author block, abstract, definitions, cited example and main
  theorem are sharp and within the margins. Overbars, subscripts and the
  Newton-polyhedron symbols are legible.
- Page 2: both lemmas, displayed parameter bounds, summation and box notation
  fit cleanly. No proof text or QED symbol is clipped.
- Page 3: primal and dual criteria are legible; the corrected nonnegative cone
  renders as `R^3_{>=0}` rather than literal letters. Proposition 4 and its
  seven-column table are aligned and fully visible.
- Page 4: the continued paragraph, theorem proof, reproducibility commands,
  disclosure and both references are readable. The page break does not orphan
  a heading or hide content.

Across all pages there are no overlaps, cropped lines, margin incursions,
missing glyphs, unreadable figures, distorted charts, or broken citations.
There are no figures or charts requiring value-by-value visual validation.
The hollow squares are the intentional theorem-proof end marks.
